PART B — THE REPLICATION BREAK-EVEN
====================================================================================================
  A local replica saves, per read:        Td + 2Tp  = 5.27 .. 14.90 ms
  A replica costs, per update:  local_update + 2Tp  = 77.43 .. 87.06 ms

  => a fragment must be read 6x to 15x more often than it is updated,
     AT THAT SITE, before a replica breaks even.
